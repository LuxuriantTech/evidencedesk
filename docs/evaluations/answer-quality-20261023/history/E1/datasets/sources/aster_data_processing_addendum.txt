ASTER DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
Parties: Aster Freight Systems Ltd. and Quill Harbor Logistics GmbH.
Effective date: 2027-02-10.

PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTICE
For a confirmed personal-data breach, Aster shall notify Quill Harbor within 12 hours after discovery.
This clause applies to personal-data breaches. This addendum does not state whether it replaces or takes precedence over the security-incident notice term in the Aster Fleet Services Agreement.

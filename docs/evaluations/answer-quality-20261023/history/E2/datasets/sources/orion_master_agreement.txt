ORION CLOUD PLATFORM — MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Parties: Orion Materials Group plc and LumenGrid Systems SAS.
Effective date: 2027-04-03.
Annual platform fee: EUR 61,400, billed in two equal instalments.
The annual security review budget is EUR 61,900 and is separate from the platform fee.

TERM, RENEWAL AND NOTICE
The agreement renews on 2028-04-03 unless a party gives written notice at least 30 days before renewal.

SECURITY EVENTS
LumenGrid shall notify Orion of a confirmed cybersecurity incident within 36 hours after confirmation.
The incident clock begins at confirmation, not when the first service alert appears.

--- PAGE 3 ---
PRODUCTION ACCESS
No subcontractor may access Orion production data before Orion gives prior written approval.

SERVICE DESK
For a routine service request, LumenGrid targets an initial response within four business hours. This service-desk target is not an incident-notification deadline.
